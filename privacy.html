Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 16, 2026.

Money Canada Uploader is a personal tool used only by its owner to upload the owner's own videos to the owner's own YouTube channels. It does not collect, sell, share or store personal data about anyone else.

What the app accesses

With the owner's permission, the app uses the YouTube upload permission to upload videos and set their titles and descriptions on the owner's channels. It does not read the owner's email, contacts, files or watch history.

What is stored

The access tokens Google issues to the owner are stored privately by the owner (on the owner's computer and as encrypted secrets in the owner's private code repository) so uploads can run automatically. They are never shared with anyone.

Sharing

No data is shared with third parties. Google user data is used only to provide the upload feature described above, in line with the Google API Services User Data Policy, including its Limited Use requirements.

Revoking access

The owner can remove the app's access at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.

Contact

Questions can be left as a comment on the YouTube channels TFSA & Money Canada or Stick Parables TV.
